## Projects

### [Design of LLM-Based Learning Content Generation and Development of Language Learning Service Features](https://github.com/2p990i9hpral/Portfolio/blob/main/projects/language_platform-EN.md)
- Designed prompts, output schemas, and constraints so that an LLM can generate learning curricula and problems.
- Compared two curriculum generation approaches (single-pass / section-by-section) in terms of response time and word duplication, and adopted the single-pass approach.
- Designed an adaptive polling schedule for asynchronous LLM jobs based on their response-time distribution.
- Implemented Anki-style SRS scheduling, a learning statistics dashboard, and supporting tools for the learning service (Lesson Importer, Navigator).

### [Development of LLM-Based Language Learning Tools](https://github.com/2p990i9hpral/LingQ_Add-On)

- Implemented AI flashcard generation, storage, and Anki sync.
